Community guidelines

Ai Talk is for respectful English practice. These rules keep rooms and match calls safe.

1. Be respectful

  • Speak kindly — no insults, bullying, or harassment
  • No hate speech based on religion, caste, gender, race, or nationality
  • No sexual content, grooming, or unwanted advances

2. Keep it practice-focused

  • Use rooms and calls to practice speaking English
  • Do not spam chat or flood rooms with nonsense
  • Do not impersonate others or fake your identity to scam people

3. Safety first

  • Do not share others’ private info without consent
  • Do not threaten, blackmail, or encourage self-harm
  • Report abuse using in-app report/block tools when available

4. No illegal activity

  • No scams, fraud, or phishing
  • No promoting drugs, weapons, or violent crime
  • No piracy or unauthorized copyrighted material dumps

5. Room hosts

  • Hosts should keep rooms orderly and remove clear abuse
  • Do not misuse mute/remove tools to harass people
  • Music should be appropriate and not copyright-abusive spam

6. Enforcement

If you break these guidelines, we may:

  • Warn you
  • Mute, remove, or block you from rooms/calls
  • Suspend or permanently ban your account

7. Report a problem

Email: teachmania@gmail.com
Website: https://teachmania.online/
Include your User ID, approximate time, and what happened.
