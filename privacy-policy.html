Privacy policy

What Ai Talk collects, why we need it, who sees it, and the choices you have.

Operator: Teachmania Private Limited · Website: teachmania.online

1. Who we are

Ai Talk is operated by Teachmania Private Limited (“we”, “us”). The app helps people practice spoken English through lessons, AI conversations, learner match calls, and live voice rooms.

Package ID: com.tm.tcghklqd
Privacy email: teachmania@gmail.com
Website: https://teachmania.online/

2. Information we collect

Account & identity

  • Email address, phone number, password
  • OTP for verification
  • Display name / full name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Sign-in token stored on your device

Learning & practice

  • Language preference and English level
  • Lesson answers and completion
  • Speech practice transcripts (“what you said”) and scores
  • AI scenario chat messages
  • Progress stats (streak, minutes, accuracy)

Rooms & match calls

  • Room title/category, seat actions, mute status
  • Room chat messages
  • Live voice audio during rooms and 1:1 calls
  • Online/offline availability and call quota
  • Ratings, feedback, and safety reports
  • Virtual coins, gifts, frames, and themes

We do not intentionally collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Camera capture (unless a future feature needs it and asks first)

4. How we use your data

  • Create and secure your account
  • Deliver lessons, AI practice, rooms, and match calls
  • Show progress and personalize learning
  • Enable live voice and chat features
  • Handle safety (mute, block, reports)
  • Send optional call/room notifications
  • Improve reliability and comply with law

6. Retention

  • Account data while your account is active
  • Chats, learning, and moderation records as needed for service and safety
  • OTPs are temporary
  • Logging out clears local session data on your device
  • You can request account/data deletion via the contact email below

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards including authenticated API access and limited provider access. No online service is fully secure — use a strong password and keep your device updated.

8. Children’s privacy

Ai Talk is for users who can legally create an account in their region. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the higher age required locally). Contact us if you believe a child has shared data — we will delete it.

9. Your rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw optional permissions in device settings
  • Log out of the app

10. Payments

If Premium purchases are enabled, payments are processed by Google Play and/or UPI apps. Those providers process payment details under their own policies. We receive what we need to unlock features.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
